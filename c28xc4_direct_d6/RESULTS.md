# Direct `C28 x C4` `[[112,16,d>=6]]` search

## Outcome

The direct search found connected `[[112,16,6]]` codes with all of the target
Ising logical properties.  It did **not** find a connected distance-six code
with a complete stabilizer presentation of maximum weight 12.  The smallest
successful maximum presentation weight in this family is 16.

The selected representative is `ideal-6c21985b5722a169`:

\[
\boxed{[[112,16,6]]}.
\]

It has exact physical self-duality,

\[
H_X=H_Z,
\]

and 16 pairwise-disjoint weight-seven logical supports forming one complete
logical `C4 x C4` grid.

## Search coverage

The physical group is `C28 x C4`.  Using `C28 = C7 x C4`, the code algebra
decomposes into a 16-dimensional trivial sector and two reciprocal
`GF(8)[C4 x C4]` sectors.  An ideal in one reciprocal sector and its
annihilator in the other always produce a rank-48 self-orthogonal binary check
space.  Hence every accepted row space has

\[
n=112,\qquad k=112-2(48)=16.
\]

The first pass combined all 70 nilpotent monomial ideals with every distinct
principal ideal generated by a normalized nonunit polynomial of at most three
`GF(8)` terms.  After deduplication there were 195 row spaces.

| Exact distance | Row spaces |
| ---: | ---: |
| 2 | 3 |
| 3 | 2 |
| 4 | 56 |
| 6 | 34 |
| 7 | 100 |

Every one of the 34 distance-six spaces was then subjected to an exact
mixed-sector weight-12 completion.  The MILP searched for a stabilizer of
weight at most 12 outside the current translation-invariant light span.  When
that span reached rank 48, a second exact MILP searched for any weight-12
stabilizer capable of enlarging its displacement subgroup.

| Weight-12 result among the 34 distance-six spaces | Count |
| --- | ---: |
| Complete rank-48 light span | 18 |
| Complete and connected light presentation | 0 |
| Final light rank 45 | 2 |
| Final light rank 42 | 4 |
| Final light rank 36 | 6 |
| Final light rank 24 | 4 |

All 18 complete weight-12 presentations are confined to an index-two subgroup
of size 56.  The connectivity MILPs certify that no other weight-12
stabilizer can connect their two components.

At weight 16, 29 of the 34 distance-six spaces have complete presentations
visible from translated light rows, and seven are connected on all 112
physical qubits.

## Logical `C4 x C4` grid

Let `u` generate `C28` and `v` generate `C4`.  The thickness subgroup is
generated by `u^4` and has order seven.  The canonical logical supports are

\[
\ell_{a,b}
=u^a v^b\left(1+u^4+u^8+u^{12}+u^{16}+u^{20}+u^{24}\right),
\qquad a,b\in C_4.
\]

These 16 supports have weight seven and are mutually disjoint.  Their binary
Gram matrix is `I_16`, they commute with every stabilizer, and they are
independent and complete modulo the stabilizer space.  The physical
translations act as

\[
u:\ell_{a,b}\mapsto\ell_{a+1,b},\qquad
v:\ell_{a,b}\mapsto\ell_{a,b+1}.
\]

Because the same supports represent logical X and Z operators and
`H_X=H_Z`, transversal physical Hadamard implements 16 independent logical
Hadamards without moving the grid into a spectator sector.  Pairwise
transversal CNOT between two identical blocks supplies the global logical
CNOT.

## Selected check space

For the representative, the reciprocal-sector ideal has the compact
description

\[
I=(U^3,V),\qquad
\operatorname{Ann}(I)=(UV^3),
\]

inside

\[
\operatorname{GF}(8)[U,V]/(U^4,V^4),\qquad U=1+X,\quad V=1+Y.
\]

Two binary group-ring seeds give a connected translation-symmetric
presentation.  Both have weight 16.  Their orbit sizes and ranks are

| Seed | Orbit size | Orbit rank | Displacement subgroup |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `h_1` | 112 | 45 | 112 |
| `h_2` | 28 | 12 | 28 |
| Combined | 140 checks | 48 | 112 |

The exact weight-12 calculation for this row space stops at rank 36 and proves
that no additional stabilizer of weight at most 12 exists outside that span.
Thus maximum check weight 16 is necessary for a complete presentation of this
specific code.

The exact distance calculation excludes logical operators through weight five
and finds the weight-six support

\[
\{0,1,16,17,80,81\}.
\]

Therefore the code distance is exactly six.  The canonical grid logicals
remain weight seven; the distance-six witness lies in another representative
of the logical space.

## Syndrome schedules

Two explicit one-CSS-type schedules are saved.

| Presentation | Ancillas | Check weights | CNOTs | Depth | Qubit degree |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Independent | 48 | 36 of weight 8; 12 of weight 16 | 480 | 16 | 2--7 |
| Translation symmetric | 140 | 140 of weight 16 | 2240 | 20 | exactly 20 |

Both depths are optimal for their displayed Tanner graphs by bipartite edge
coloring.  The independent schedule is substantially cheaper, but its column
degrees are not uniformly even.  The redundant translation-symmetric schedule
has even degree 20 on every qubit and therefore retains the global even
syndrome-parity relation useful for one-round STAR postselection.

The check space is doubly even.  In addition to transversal Hadamard, this
retains the usual transversal-phase compatibility of a doubly-even
self-dual CSS presentation.

## Scope of the result

The weight-12 exclusion is exact for all 34 distance-six row spaces found by
this monomial-plus-sparse-principal ideal search.  It is not a theorem about
every possible ideal of `GF(8)[C4 x C4]`, nor about more general GALA
protographs or nonabelian lifts.

The new distance-six representative does not improve the maximum check weight
over the previously saved `[[112,16,7]]` code.  Its practical advantage is a
lighter independent measurement basis: 480 rather than 768 CNOTs per CSS
type, at the same optimal depth 16.
